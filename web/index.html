    This works out the climate, lets rain run from the mountains to the sea, cuts valleys, fills lakes and colours the land by its biome. It runs on your computer and replaces the map's picture until you next change something.

    It needs a reasonably recent computer: about 2 to 4 seconds at 1024 working resolution and 10 to 20 seconds at 2048, with around 1 GB of free memory at 2048. Older or low-powered devices will take longer. Your map isn't changed, and you can keep editing afterwards.
